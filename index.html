BEFORE A CAR

A lab concept with a review route.

The FIA rules prescribe the driver voice system and restrict duplicating standard components. An on-car design needs the designated supplier and FIA process. The computer bench is a way to investigate behavior before hardware work.

THE REASON WE STARTED / 03

A winning drive.
A voice going missing.

Max Verstappen won in Singapore while Red Bull struggled to hear his replies. The result survived. The conversation needed improvisation.

Race engineer Gianpiero “GP” Lambiase wanted Max’s thoughts about a possible Safety Car pit stop, but hearing him was difficult. The team also used the pit board. Afterwards, Max described questions in code, a radio that had troubled him throughout the season, and a joke that GP might have enjoyed the quiet.

That became our starting point: what could keep the driver’s voice available when a primary capture path fails? We explored separate microphones and wiring, then built a bench where those decisions could be tested without buying hardware.

The app makes both the promise and the limitation visible. A backup can keep the local signal flowing. A shared output failure can still defeat both inputs.

The exact failed component in Max’s car was not established in the reports we checked. This bench models possible faults and has no affiliation with Formula 1, the FIA, Red Bull Racing, or Max Verstappen.
